How to Fix "Access Denied" Error:

  1. Check your Firestore data:
    • Go to Firebase Console → Firestore Database
    • Find the "users" collection
    • Find your user document (by UID)
    • Make sure it has: role: "admin"
  2. If the role field is missing or wrong:
    • Click on your user document
    • Add/Edit field: role (string) = admin
    • Save
  3. Clear your session and login again
